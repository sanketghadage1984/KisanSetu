You are a senior presentation designer and pitching coach for the Smart India Hackathon (SIH).
Create a winning, highly impactful 10-12 slide pitch deck presentation for an innovative AgriTech platform named "KisanSetu" (कृषि सेतु — The Direct Digital Bridge Between Farmers & Buyers).

Theme: Empowering Indian Farmers, Eliminating Middlemen, and Securing Farm-to-Fork Value Chains.
Tone: Visionary, data-backed, empathetic to rural reality, and technologically advanced.
Color Palette: Agri-Green (#2D6A4F), Golden Wheat (#D4A373), Cream Background, Clean Slate Accents.

Include the following slide-by-slide structure with detailed bullet points, statistics, and architectural highlights:

SLIDE 1: Title & Hero
- Project Name: KisanSetu (किसान सेतु)
- Tagline: "Empowering Annadata with AI, Fair Prices & Direct Market Access"
- Hackathon: Smart India Hackathon 2024
- Team Name & Problem Statement ID
- Key Metrics: 0% Middlemen Commission | Up to 35% Higher Farmer Realization | Real-Time Mandi Integration

SLIDE 2: The Ground Reality (Problem Statement)
- High Middlemen Exploitation: Up to 30-40% of crop value lost to APMC intermediaries.
- Information Asymmetry: Farmers lack real-time price awareness across distant mandis.
- Post-Harvest Spoilage: Perishables degrade rapidly due to delayed transit and poor storage planning.
- Rural Connectivity Barrier: Spotty internet in tier-3/4 villages prevents access to complex web apps.
- Trust Deficit: Payment defaults and delayed settlements create hesitation to trade outside local traders.

SLIDE 3: The Solution — KisanSetu Ecosystem
- An all-in-one Progressive Web App (PWA) operating seamlessly offline and online.
- Direct digital bridge connecting farmers with verified traders, institutional buyers, and FPOs.
- Transparent price discovery, AI-driven logistics estimation, and smart contract escrow protection.

SLIDE 4: Core Innovation 1 — AI-Powered Post-Harvest Intelligence
- AI Spoilage Risk Predictor: Evaluates crop shelf-life decay, local ambient temperatures, and travel hours to estimate spoilage losses in ₹ and suggest optimal harvest dispatch windows.
- AI Smart Transport Cost Estimator: Auto-recommends vehicle class (Tempo vs Mini Truck vs Heavy Truck), computes diesel index, road condition penalties, and cost per kg to calculate true net farmer realization.
- AI 7-Day Price Forecast: Moving averages and seasonal demand indicators to advise farmers: "Sell Now" vs "Hold 3-5 Days".

SLIDE 5: Core Innovation 2 — Live Mandi Data & Shree Anna (Millet) Focus
- data.gov.in Integration: Live daily wholesale prices from 100+ APMCs across India, with intelligent 6-hour caching for zero latency.
- Dedicated Shree Anna (Millet) Mission Corridor: Special support for climate-resilient superfoods (Ragi, Jowar, Bajra, Foxtail Millet) to support Government of India's global millet push.

SLIDE 6: Core Innovation 3 — Trust, Escrow & Blockchain Traceability
- Digital Escrow Payment Flow: Eliminates payment defaults — Trader locks funds in smart contract -> Crop dispatched -> Farmer confirms delivery -> Instant payout released.
- Blockchain Farm-to-Fork Provenance: SHA-256 cryptographic verification for every harvest batch with printable QR code for consumers and export-grade verification.
- Visual Live Transit Tracking: Step-by-step shipment status from farmgate to buyer warehouse.

SLIDE 7: Inclusivity & Grassroots Accessibility
- PWA (Progressive Web App): Installable like a native app on basic Android smartphones; works offline with background sync.
- Multilingual Support: Localized in Hindi (हिंदी), Marathi (मराठी), and English with dialect-friendly terminology.
- Voice-First Navigation: Speech-to-text search designed for illiterate and neo-literate farmers.
- Government Scheme Matcher: One-click eligibility checker for 10+ central schemes (PM-KISAN, PMFBY, PMFME, AIF).

SLIDE 8: System Architecture & Tech Stack
- Frontend: High-performance Vanilla JS & CSS design system (zero heavy bundle bloat for 2G/3G speeds).
- PWA Engine: Service Worker with Cache-First asset handling and IndexedDB persistence.
- Backend & Cloud: Python proxy service for data.gov.in APIs, Firebase Auth & Firestore for real-time negotiations.
- Intelligence: OpenMeteo live agro-meteorological forecasting & custom deterministic ML heuristics.

SLIDE 9: Market Opportunity & Business Model
- Target Audience: 140M+ Indian farming households, 50,000+ registered FPOs, verified commercial traders.
- Revenue Model:
  1. Micro-transaction fee on finalized institutional contracts (0.5% - 1%).
  2. Premium logistics & cold-chain booking partner commissions.
  3. B2B analytics & verified supply chain verification APIs for FMCG enterprises.

SLIDE 10: Social Impact & Alignment with Government Vision
- Aligned with: PM-KISAN, e-NAM expansion, Atmanirbhar Bharat, and International Year of Millets.
- Reducing carbon footprint via optimized transport routing and minimizing post-harvest food waste.
- Increasing rural household incomes by directly returning trading margins to farmers.

SLIDE 11: Roadmap & Future Scale
- Phase 1 (Achieved): PWA, AI Spoilage & Transport modules, Live Mandi integration, Escrow UI, Blockchain hashes.
- Phase 2 (Next 6 Months): Direct UPI Auto-Pay integration, drone-based crop health assessment integration, multi-mandi pooling for smallholders.
- Phase 3 (12 Months): Cross-border export compliance for FPOs and organic certification traceability.

SLIDE 12: Conclusion & Q&A
